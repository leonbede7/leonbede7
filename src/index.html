The decisions
behind the interface.

The part I’m proudest of is its purpose. Building for my family’s dealership gave every design and technical decision a real context.

Make the workflow clear.

I mapped inventory, inquiries and trade-ins before shaping the screens. The goal was a connected operating workflow with usable public entry points.

My role: business context, requirements, product direction, design review and iteration.

Give AI a defined role.

DeepSeek assists vehicle-data extraction and reply drafts. Structured output is checked and presented for review. A draft does not send itself, and a plausible answer is not automatically a valid record.

Design the access boundary.

Public browsing and protected operations share a controlled data layer. Authentication, data-access policies, validation, monitoring and automated checks support that separation.

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Supabase/Postgres, DeepSeek and Vercel.

Own the result, keep learning.

AI generated much of the implementation under my direction. I defined the work, reviewed outputs, tested behavior and iterated. The project taught me how design, CRM, security and delivery fit together.

The product is live. I do not yet have measured time-saving or sales-impact results to report.
